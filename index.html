How it works

From blank page to broadcast in four steps.

1

Type your script

Paste any text — a sentence, a chapter, a podcast intro. Inline markup like [pause] and {emphasis} is supported.

2

Pick or mix a voice

Choose from 50+ voices or blend two together with the mixer. Save your favourite blends to your private voice library.

3

Style with one click

Select an emotion preset, tweak the intensity slider and toggle Pro DSP — every change auto-renders the audio in real time.

4

Export & ship

Download as 24-bit WAV, 320 kbps MP3 or lossless FLAC. Use it in your video, podcast, game or app — royalty-free.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Does any audio or text leave my computer?

No. Wave Voice Studio runs entirely in your browser using ONNX Runtime Web. The model and voice files are downloaded once from a public CDN and cached locally. After that, every generation, mix and effect happens on your device — no requests are made to our servers.

Can I use the generated audio commercially?

Yes. Audio you generate with Wave Voice Studio is royalty-free for both personal and commercial use, including ads, podcasts, games, films and audiobooks. You don't need to credit us, but a mention is always appreciated.

What browsers are supported?

Any modern Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc) gives you the fastest experience via WebGPU. Safari and Firefox work too with the WebAssembly fallback — slightly slower on first generation but indistinguishable in audio quality.

How big is the download?

About 83 MB for the default Balanced quality tier — downloaded once and cached forever. Lite mode (~42 MB) is available for slow connections; Studio FP16 (~155 MB) is offered for users who want zero compromise on fidelity.

How is this different from cloud TTS services?

No accounts, no API keys, no per-character charges, no rate limits, no waiting on a network round-trip and — most importantly — your scripts never touch our servers. The trade-off is the one-time model download; after that you have an unlimited TTS engine in your pocket.

Can I install it like a real app?

Yes — Wave Voice Studio is a Progressive Web App. In Chrome, click the install icon in the address bar; in Safari on iOS/iPadOS, use Share → Add to Home Screen. It then runs in its own window, fully offline.
